### Separate physical quoted-LOAD probe
With this unchanged classic keyboard routine and the harness's physical matrix path, Shift+quote does not reliably enter the required quotation marks. The observed command 'LOAD 2ROMRUNNER,8,1' reports ?SYNTAX ERROR. The successful game load above therefore uses an explicitly queued PETSCII command. Its IEC transfer and subsequent RUN/game controls still execute in the real core.
